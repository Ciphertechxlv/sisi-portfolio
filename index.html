Goodnight From Home

A PEEK INSIDE

"Ọlọ́run á sọ ọ́ di ọlọ́rọ̀ ní ọjọ́ ọ̀la," Grandma would whisper, tucking the blanket beneath his chin. Tonight, she is far away — but her lullaby travels the distance easily, carried on Aunty Sisi's voice instead.

A bedtime story built from real memory — Grandma's Yoruba lullaby, Mama's whispered prayers, and a promise made to a nephew who wanted a story of his very own.
